## Known Limitations (By Design)

### 1. Kafka Not Included in Render Deployment
**Status:** ⚠️ EXPECTED
**Impact:** Event streaming features will log warnings but app will work
**Reason:** Render doesn't provide managed Kafka
**Solution:** Can add Upstash Kafka later if needed

### 2. Free Tier Resource Limits
**Status:** ⚠️ EXPECTED
**Impact:**
- Web service: Spins down after 15min inactivity
- PostgreSQL: 256MB RAM, 1GB storage, expires after 90 days
- Redis: 25MB RAM, expires after 90 days

**Mitigation:**
- Use UptimeRobot to keep web service awake
- Upgrade to paid plans when needed ($21/month total)

### 3. Limited Cache Size
**Status:** ⚠️ EXPECTED
**Impact:** Lower cache hit rate (~40-60% vs 85%+)
**Reason:** Redis only has 25MB on free tier
**Solution:** Upgrade Redis to standard plan ($25/mo for 256MB)

---

## Performance Expectations

### Free Tier Performance:
- **Response Time:** 50-100ms (vs 15ms with more RAM)
- **Concurrent Users:** ~50
- **Cache Hit Rate:** 40-60%
- **Cold Start:** 30-60 seconds after sleep

### After Upgrading to Paid Tier:
- **Response Time:** 15-20ms
- **Concurrent Users:** 500+
- **Cache Hit Rate:** 85%+
- **No Cold Starts:** Always-on service
